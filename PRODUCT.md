# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Developers, product managers, designers, and AI engineers attending ClawCon who want to learn about practical agent engineering.

## Product Purpose
A presentation slide deck communicating rational, safe, and trustworthy design principles for integrating LLM agents into enterprise products.

## Brand Personality
Technical, authoritative, crisp, rational, print-editorial yet terminal-inspired.

## Anti-references
- SaaS landing-page clichés (neon gradients, 3D bubbly illustrations, generic dashboard screenshots).
- Oversimplified pastel presentations.
- Sloppy typography and cramped layouts.

## Design Principles
- **No Font Smaller Than 24px:** Absolute legibility on a projector.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Use code snippets, terminal mocks, and clear diagrams.
- **Information Density:** Balance tight tracking on headers with relaxed line-height on body text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast requirements for projector display.
- Large minimum font sizes.
